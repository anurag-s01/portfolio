## The problem

Every promotion change used to need an engineering release. Marketing waited on deploy windows, and engineers spent time shipping config that should have been data.

## What I built

A multi-tenant Campaign Engine in Java and Spring Boot with a custom rule-evaluation core:

- **Complex condition schemas**: campaigns are expressed as nested conditions over user, cart, city and time, evaluated against the live cart.
- **Priority-based execution**: overlapping campaigns resolve deterministically by priority.
- **Multi-dimensional cohort targeting**: target by user segment, geography and behaviour at once.
- **Multi-tenant by design**: one platform serves multiple brands and campaign types.

## Impact

- **100+ campaign changes per quarter** absorbed with **zero deployments**.
- Launched personalized packaging and COD charge campaigns on the platform, lifting **new-user conversion roughly 16%** over the pre-launch baseline in the first month.
