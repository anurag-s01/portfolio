## The problem

Domino's India talked to customers through 11+ isolated vendor integrations with no fallback. If a vendor degraded, messages were simply lost, and every team re-implemented templates and tracking.

## What I built

A multi-tenant, event-driven platform on Kafka and Spring Boot, now adopted by all 5 JFL brands:

- **Five channels**: SMS, Push, Email, WhatsApp and Live Activities behind one API.
- **Template management** shared across brands and teams.
- **Multi-vendor fallback** so a degraded provider no longer means lost messages.
- **Every request tracked** for billing and audit.

## Making the hot path fast

I reduced MongoDB query latency on the notification hot path from **18.7ms to 3.9ms (79%)** by profiling queries, redesigning indexes and trimming projections. Traffic later grew on the same infrastructure.

## Impact

- Handles **millions of requests a day**, peaking at **134K per minute**.
