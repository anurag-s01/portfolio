## The problem

When a store gets overloaded, every minute of manual reaction means late orders. Mitigation across a fleet of 2,500+ stores could not depend on people watching dashboards.

## What I built

A real-time evaluation platform that:

- **Ingests live customer, rider and order events** through Kafka.
- **Scores per-store load** against a predictive model.
- **Triggers graduated mitigations** over RabbitMQ, escalating only as far as the load requires.

## Impact

- Operational-load mitigation is **automated across 2,500+ stores**.
