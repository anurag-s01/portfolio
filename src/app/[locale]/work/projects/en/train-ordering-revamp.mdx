## Overview

Two main pieces of work, alongside other optimisations to the train ordering flow.

## Zomato train orders

I integrated Zomato for train orders, so passengers can order Domino's on Zomato and have it delivered to their seat on the train. The integration is template-driven, which keeps the partner-specific parts in configuration rather than code.

## Order management with NXG cart

I revamped how the order management service integrates with NXG cart for train orders. This was a backend optimisation: the customer flow stays the same, the plumbing underneath got simpler and faster.

## Impact

- Train ordering flow conversion up **230 bps**.
- Train order volume up **25%**, with Zomato as a new channel.
