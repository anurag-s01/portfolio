## The problem

A service on the ordering path peaks at 155K requests per minute. At that volume, every millisecond of p99 compounds into the calls that depend on it.

## What I changed

- **Removed an N+1 query** that issued a lookup per item instead of one batched read.
- **Added an in-memory cache** for data that rarely changes but was read on every request.

## Impact

- **p99 latency**: 29.4ms → **5.8ms** (−80%)
- **Average latency**: 14.6ms → **2.6ms**
