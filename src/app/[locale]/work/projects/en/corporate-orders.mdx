## The problem

Corporate orders were taken offline and handled manually, which capped how many large orders the business could take and made every one of them slow.

## What I built

A self-serve platform for corporate clients that:

- Takes orders of **up to 1,000 unique items in any quantity**.
- Does it **without adding load to store systems**.

## Impact

- Removed manual handling from corporate fulfillment.
- Opened a scalable B2B ordering channel.
