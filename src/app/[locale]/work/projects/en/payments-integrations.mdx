## What I built

**New payment methods at checkout**

- **TWID pay-by-rewards**: customers pay with loyalty points, as a new tender that works alongside cash, card, UPI and wallet.
- **eVoucher**: a single eVoucher option for customers, backed by two providers (Razorpay and VIPL). Which provider handles a voucher is an internal detail the customer never sees.

**Swiggy Toing as an ordering partner**

Toing, a Swiggy sub-brand, is an aggregator. I integrated it with Domino's ordering backend so customers can place Domino's orders, or orders for other JFL brands, from Toing.

## Impact

- **4+ payment and partner integrations** added to Domino's ordering platform.
- The TWID pay-by-rewards integration earned a **Silver Award**.
